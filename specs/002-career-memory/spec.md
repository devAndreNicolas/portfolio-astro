# Career memory and truthful tailoring

## Outcome

Give agents a private, evidence-indexed profile of André's experience and a separate 2026 market-positioning reference, so future CVs can be tailored consistently without inventing credentials or outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria

- The profile records each reusable professional claim with a source and a limitation where relevant.
- Current CV inventory matches files that exist.
- Market signals are explicitly separate from candidate claims.
- The career-tailoring skill permits faithful translation and framing but rejects fabricated institutions, revenue, employment, titles, customers, and outcomes.

## Non-goals

- Declaring unsupported abilities as skills.
- Replacing evidence collection from applications or interviews.
