# Plan

`career/profile/master-career.md` is the private seam between raw artifacts and tailored documents. Its interface is evidence IDs, source locations, safe positioning, and explicit development targets. CVs and agents consume this profile; they do not infer facts from market trends.

`career/market/2026-positioning.md` is a separate adapter for time-sensitive hiring context. It may change the selection or ordering of proven claims, but cannot add a claim.
