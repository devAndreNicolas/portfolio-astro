Job Summary

You will be one of the senior engineers building a new headless front-end for one of the most visited news sites in the world. The team has an aggressive goal of launching a first page early next year. You will shape the architecture, set front-end standards, and work with AI-assisted workflows in the news media industry.

Responsibilities

Architect & Build: Lead the design and implementation of a modern, headless front-end architecture for a high-traffic news platform reaching millions globally.

Ship High-Impact Milestones: Drive the timely execution of key front-end releases to meet ambitious, fast-paced launch goals early next year.

Establish Technical Excellence: Define and advocate front-end engineering standards across code quality, component design patterns, testing, and code reviews.

Optimize Web Performance: Direct ownership of Core Web Vitals, SEO, accessibility (a11y), and page speed performance for all user-facing surfaces.

Modern Integration: Integrate scalable headless CMS solutions with next-generation in-house backend APIs.

Technical Leadership: Mentor mid-level engineers and partner closely with engineering leadership to drive architectural direction.

AI-Driven Innovation: Leverage and continuously refine AI-assisted and agentic workflows within the engineering lifecycle.

Qualifications and Job Requirements

5+ Years Experience: Senior-level front-end ownership on production systems.

React & Next.js: Strong production experience with SSR, SSG, ISR, and App Router.

TypeScript: Fluent in writing clean, type-safe TypeScript as the main language.

Headless CMS & APIs: Proven experience connecting front-ends with headless CMS and APIs.

Performance at Scale: Strong grasp of Core Web Vitals, CDNs, caching, and asset optimization.

AI-First Workflows: Daily usage of AI coding tools and agentic workflows.

Fluent English: Professional English for daily work with a US team.
