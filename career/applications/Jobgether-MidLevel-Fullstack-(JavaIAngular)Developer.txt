This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Mid-Level Fullstack (Java | Angular) Developer based in Brazil.

This is an opportunity to contribute to full-stack software development within a collaborative, agile environment serving the financial sector.

You will work across backend and frontend technologies, building scalable solutions with Java, Angular, Node.js, microservices, and REST APIs.

The role combines hands-on development with a focus on engineering quality, architecture, and continuous improvement.

You will collaborate with multidisciplinary teams to translate business and user needs into efficient technical solutions.

There is also an opportunity to support junior developers through guidance and knowledge sharing.

AI-assisted development tools are part of the engineering toolkit, supporting productivity and modern development practices.

The position is remote within Brazil, with specific office attendance requirements for professionals located in the Campinas metropolitan region.

Accountabilities

    Develop and evolve full-stack applications using Java, Angular, Node.js, microservices, and REST APIs, with a focus on scalability, reliability, and user experience. 
    Contribute to continuous improvement of engineering processes, development practices, architecture, and technical solutions. 
    Design and implement solutions based on microservices and microfrontend architectures, applying patterns such as BFF and MVVM where appropriate. 
    Apply software engineering principles and development practices including SOLID, KISS, YAGNI, DRY, and DDD. 
    Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams throughout the design, development, testing, and implementation lifecycle. 
    Participate actively in agile ceremonies and Scrum practices, contributing to planning, delivery, and continuous improvement. 
    Provide technical guidance and support to junior developers, helping promote knowledge sharing and stronger engineering practices across the team. 
    Use AI-assisted development tools such as Flow, Claude, and Copilot to improve development workflows and productivity. 
    Contribute, when applicable, to architecture, CI/CD, Docker, networking, and automated testing initiatives. 

Requirements

    Professional experience developing applications with Java, Angular, Node.js, microservices, and REST APIs. 
    Practical experience with microservices and microfrontend architectures. 
    Knowledge of architectural patterns such as Backend for Frontend (BFF) and MVVM. 
    Strong understanding of software development principles and practices, including SOLID, KISS, YAGNI, DRY, and Domain-Driven Design (DDD). 
    Experience working in agile environments, particularly with Scrum methodologies. 
    Familiarity with Git and GitHub for version control and collaborative development. 
    Familiarity with AI-powered development tools such as Flow, Claude, or GitHub Copilot. 
    Strong collaboration, communication, critical-thinking, and problem-solving skills. 
    Ability to focus on the business and user impact of technical solutions rather than development alone. 
    Experience with architecture, CI/CD, Docker, networking, and automated testing is desirable.
