# Market positioning signals — October 2026

This is job-analysis context, not candidate evidence. Do not copy any item into a CV unless it is separately supported in `career/profile/master-career.md`.

## Signals relevant to André's direction

- Software-engineering hiring is increasingly pairing traditional engineering with cloud and AI-related skills, while web fundamentals remain relevant. [LinkedIn Economic Graph, February 2026](https://economicgraph.linkedin.com/content/dam/me/economicgraph/en-us/PDF/us-software-engineer-talent-landscape-2026.pdf)
- AI and big data, networks/cybersecurity, and technological literacy are among the fastest-growing skill areas identified by employers; human-centred skills remain important alongside GenAI. [World Economic Forum skills outlook](https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/in-full/3-skills-outlook/)
- Agentic coding shifts differentiation toward systems understanding, codebase context, testing, review, and engineering judgment rather than prompt-only claims. [Gartner, April 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/7779753)

## How to apply this

Prioritize evidence that connects a user/problem, a constraint, the technical decision, quality controls, and an outcome. For AI-oriented roles, distinguish:

1. AI-assisted development workflow (already evidenced);
2. user-facing LLM feature work with safeguards (already evidenced through Genkit); and
3. RAG, agent orchestration, evaluation, observability, and governance (development targets until evidenced).

For product-facing roles, use the existing evidence of requirements, UX, scope, conversion-oriented flows, and stakeholder collaboration. Do not claim sales ownership, revenue, pricing, or investment validation without direct evidence.
