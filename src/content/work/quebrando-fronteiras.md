## Context

Quebrando Fronteiras is a social impact platform focused on donation and fundraising journeys.

## Problem

Critical user paths had friction points that affected completion behavior in donation-related interactions.

## Role & Ownership

I refactored key frontend flows and interaction patterns to improve usability in high-intent moments.

## Technical Decisions

- Reworked critical journeys with clearer flow progression and reduced interaction friction.
- Improved mobile behavior for donation-related steps.
- Used analytics-informed iteration to guide UX adjustments.
- Kept implementation maintainable in Next.js + TypeScript.

## Outcome

- Donation flow interactions became clearer and easier to complete.
- Usability improvements supported stronger conversion-oriented behavior.

## Notes / Lessons

Small UX improvements in critical funnels can produce outsized impact in social products.
